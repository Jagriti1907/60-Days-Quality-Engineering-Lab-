60 DAYS | QUALITY ENGINEERING LAB

FROM THEORY TO REAL-WORLD INDUSTRIAL APPLICATIONS

ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

60 Days | Quality Engineering Lab is an independent engineering initiative focused on exploring, understanding, and applying Quality Engineering methodologies through structured technical experiments and industrial case studies.

The initiative is designed around one fundamental objective: bridging the gap between engineering theory and real-world industrial applications.

Quality Engineering is not limited to product inspection or defect detection. It involves systematic process control, failure prevention, reliability improvement, risk assessment, statistical analysis, manufacturing optimization, and continuous improvement.

Throughout this 60-day journey, different engineering methodologies and industrial quality tools are explored through practical scenarios, technical calculations, analytical studies, and structured laboratory documentation.

Each experiment is developed to demonstrate how engineering principles can be translated into measurable industrial improvements.

This repository serves as the central technical documentation and knowledge base for the complete 60-day initiative.

---

PROJECT VISION

To develop a practical understanding of how Quality Engineering methodologies are applied across modern manufacturing and industrial environments.

The initiative aims to move beyond textbook explanations and explore the engineering logic behind industrial quality decisions.

The central philosophy is:

«Don't just learn quality tools. Understand the industrial problem, apply the methodology, analyze the results, and engineer the improvement.»
